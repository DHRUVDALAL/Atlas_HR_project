## SLIDE 2: Introduction
**Headline:** Your Role in ATLAS
**Content:** 
As an HR Admin, you are the core operator of the ATLAS system. You manage the entire recruitment pipeline—from reviewing incoming applications and scheduling interviews to triggering automated emails and processing final offer letters.

## SLIDE 3: System Login
**Headline:** Accessing the HR Portal
**Content:**
1. Navigate to the ATLAS secure portal.
2. Enter your assigned Corporate Email ID.
3. Enter your secure Password.
4. Click "Login".

## SLIDE 4: The HR Master Dashboard
**Headline:** Overview & Analytics
**Content:**
The HR Dashboard gives you a bird's-eye view of the recruitment pipeline.
- **Pipeline Metrics:** Track total applicants, interviews scheduled, and offers extended.
- **Recent Activity:** See real-time updates of candidates arriving at reception or interviewers submitting feedback.
- **Action Items:** Quick alerts for applications requiring your immediate screening.

---
## SLIDE 5: Screening Applications
**Headline:** Managing New Candidates
**Content:**
When a candidate submits an application on the landing page:
1. Navigate to the **"Applications"** tab.
2. Review their resume and details.
3. Update their status to **"Shortlisted for L1"** or **"Rejected"**.
4. The system will automatically send a status update email to the candidate based on your decision.

---
## SLIDE 6: Scheduling Interviews
**Headline:** Assigning the Technical Panel
**Content:**
For shortlisted candidates:
1. Click **"Schedule Interview"** on the candidate's profile.
2. Select the Date, Time, and Interview Mode (In-person / Virtual).
3. Assign an **Interviewer (L1 Panel)** from the dropdown list.
4. Click Save. The system will automatically email the candidate with the interview details and notify the assigned panel member.

---
## SLIDE 7: Reviewing Feedback
**Headline:** Making Data-Driven Decisions
**Content:**
Once an interview is completed:
1. Navigate to the **"Interview Feedback"** section.
2. Read the detailed notes and scores submitted by the L1 Panel.
3. Review their final recommendation (Shortlist/Reject).
4. Decide to either schedule an L2 interview, extend an offer, or reject the candidate.

---
## SLIDE 8: Offers & Document Verification
**Headline:** Finalizing the Hire
**Content:**
For selected candidates:
1. Move the candidate to the **"Offer Extended"** stage.
2. Use the system to generate and track the Offer Letter.
3. Track the candidate's document uploads (e.g., ID proofs, past experience letters).
4. Once documents are verified, finalize their Joining Date.

---
## SLIDE 9: Automated Emails
**Headline:** Communication at Scale
**Content:**
ATLAS handles candidate communication automatically!
Emails are sent automatically when:
- An application is received.
- An interview is scheduled or rescheduled.
- A final offer is extended.
- Documents are requested.
*Always ensure candidate email addresses are accurate to prevent delivery failures.*
