## SLIDE 2: Introduction
**Headline:** Your Role in ATLAS
**Content:** 
As a System Administrator (or Founder/MD), you have the highest level of access in the ATLAS ecosystem. You are responsible for overseeing the entire platform, managing employee accounts, configuring roles, and accessing top-level analytics to drive organizational decisions.

---
## SLIDE 3: Secure Login
**Headline:** Executive Access
**Content:**
1. Navigate to the ATLAS secure portal.
2. Enter your assigned Corporate Email ID.
3. Enter your secure Password.
4. Click "Login".
*Note: Your account has unrestricted access. Ensure your credentials are strictly confidential.*

---
## SLIDE 4: The Executive Dashboard
**Headline:** High-Level Analytics
**Content:**
The System Admin Dashboard provides critical insights:
- **Total Pipeline Overview:** Active candidates vs. Hires.
- **System Activity:** Real-time metrics on HR actions and interview completions.
- **Conversion Rates:** Analytics on how efficiently candidates move through the hiring stages.

---
## SLIDE 5: User Management (Adding Employees)
**Headline:** Scaling Your Team
**Content:**
To grant a new employee access to ATLAS:
1. Navigate to the **"User Management"** tab.
2. Click **"Add New User"**.
3. Enter the employee's details (First Name, Last Name, Corporate Email).
4. Assign a strict Role (e.g., HR_ADMIN, L1_PANEL, RECEPTIONIST).
5. Set a temporary password and click **"Create User"**.

---
## SLIDE 6: Role & Permission Assignment
**Headline:** Enforcing Security
**Content:**
Understanding System Roles:
- **RECEPTIONIST:** Can only view arriving candidates and mark attendance.
- **L1_PANEL:** Can only view candidates scheduled for *their* interviews and submit feedback.
- **HR_ADMIN:** Can view all candidates, schedule interviews, and manage offers.
- **SYSTEM_ADMIN:** Full access to everything, including User Management.
*Never grant SYSTEM_ADMIN access unless absolutely necessary.*

---
## SLIDE 7: Account Maintenance
**Headline:** Editing & Revoking Access
**Content:**
When an employee leaves the company or changes roles:
1. Navigate to **"User Management"**.
2. Search for the employee's name.
3. Click on their profile.
4. You can either **Edit** their role or change their "Status" to **Inactive** to instantly revoke their login access.

---
## SLIDE 8: System Troubleshooting
**Headline:** IT & System Escalation
**Content:**
- **Email Delivery Issues:** Verify the SMTP settings in the server environment variables.
- **Database Errors:** Check the PM2 server logs on the Ubuntu backend.
- **Forgot Password?** You can manually reset any user's password from the User Management dashboard.
