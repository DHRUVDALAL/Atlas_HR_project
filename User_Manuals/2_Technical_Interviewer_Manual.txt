## SLIDE 2: Introduction
**Headline:** Your Role in ATLAS
**Content:** 
As a Technical Interviewer (L1 Panel), you are responsible for assessing the skills and cultural fit of our candidates. The ATLAS system provides you with a clean, organized dashboard of your upcoming interviews, direct access to candidate resumes, and a streamlined evaluation form to submit your feedback directly to HR.

## SLIDE 3: System Login
**Headline:** Secure Access
**Content:**

## SLIDE 4: Your Interview Dashboard
**Headline:** Managing Your Schedule
**Content:**
Upon logging in, you will see your personalized Interview Dashboard.
- **Upcoming Interviews:** A chronological list of candidates scheduled for you today.
- **Status Indicators:** See at a glance if a candidate has "Arrived" (marked by the Receptionist) or is "Pending".
- **History:** Access your past evaluations and completed interviews.

---
## SLIDE 5: Reviewing Candidate Profiles
**Headline:** Preparing for the Interview
**Content:**
Before the interview begins:
1. Click on the scheduled candidate's name in your Dashboard.
2. Review their Profile Overview (Experience, Skills, Education).
3. Click the **"View Resume"** button to open their uploaded document securely within the browser.
4. Review any pre-screening notes left by the HR team.

---
## SLIDE 6: The Evaluation Form
**Headline:** Conducting the Interview
**Content:**
During or immediately after the interview, use the built-in Evaluation Form:
1. **Technical Skills:** Rate the candidate on specific technical competencies.
2. **Communication:** Rate their clarity and interpersonal skills.
3. **Detailed Notes:** Use the text box to provide detailed, objective feedback on their performance, strengths, and areas of concern.

---
## SLIDE 7: Submitting Your Recommendation
**Headline:** Finalizing the Feedback
**Content:**
At the end of your evaluation:
1. Select your final recommendation: **"Shortlist"** or **"Reject"**.
2. Click the **"Submit Evaluation"** button.
3. Once submitted, your feedback is instantly locked and routed securely to the HR Admin for the final hiring decision.

---
## SLIDE 8: Best Practices & Support
**Headline:** Interview Guidelines
**Content:**
- Submit evaluations within 2 hours of the interview completion to maintain workflow speed.
- Ensure all notes are objective, professional, and clear.
- **Need Help?** For technical issues, contact the IT Administrator. For scheduling conflicts, reach out to HR immediately.
