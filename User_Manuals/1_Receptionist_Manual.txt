## SLIDE 2: Introduction
**Headline:** Your Role in ATLAS
**Content:** 
As a Receptionist, you are the first point of contact for candidates arriving for their interviews. The ATLAS system empowers you to quickly verify candidate identities, mark their attendance in real-time, and automatically notify the HR team that the candidate has arrived.

---
## SLIDE 3: System Login
**Headline:** Accessing the Portal
**Content:**
1. Open your web browser and navigate to the ATLAS secure portal.
2. Enter your assigned Corporate Email ID.
3. Enter your secure Password.
4. Click "Login".

## SLIDE 4: The Reception Dashboard
**Headline:** Navigating Your Workspace
**Content:**
Upon logging in, you will see the Reception Dashboard.
- **Today's Interviews:** A quick summary of how many candidates are expected today.
- **Recent Arrivals:** A log of candidates who have already checked in.
- **Search Bar:** Located at the top of the screen to quickly find incoming candidates.

---
## SLIDE 5: Verifying a Candidate
**Headline:** Candidate Check-In Process
**Content:**
When a candidate arrives at the front desk:
1. Ask the candidate for their unique **Application Reference Number** (sent to them via email) or their full name.
2. Enter the number or name into the Search Bar.
3. Click on the candidate's profile to open their details.
4. Verify their physical Government ID against the details on the screen.

---
## SLIDE 6: Marking Attendance
**Headline:** Completing the Arrival
**Content:**
Once the candidate's identity is verified:
1. Click the **"Mark Arrived"** button on their profile.
2. The system will immediately update their status to "Waiting for Interview".
3. An automated notification is instantly sent to the HR Admin and the assigned Interview Panel.
4. Direct the candidate to the designated waiting area.

---
## SLIDE 7: Support & Troubleshooting
**Headline:** Need Assistance?
**Content:**
- **Forgot Password?** Use the "Reset Password" link on the login screen.
- **Candidate Not Found?** Ensure the candidate applied using the correct portal or contact HR immediately.
- **System Issues?** Contact the internal IT Support team or the System Administrator.
